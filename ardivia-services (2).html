Interior
Architecture

Interior architecture is not decoration. It is the spatial organisation of how a building works inside — the proportion of rooms, the flow between them, the relationship between light and surface. We do not distinguish between "architecture" and "interiors." They are one discipline.

  • Space planning and spatial sequencing
  • Material and finish specification throughout
  • Bespoke joinery design and detailing
  • Kitchen and bathroom architecture
  • Furniture layout and proportion studies
  • Colour, texture, and palette development

06 — Windows & Doors
06 — Expanded Service

Windows & Doors
— Restoration &
Bespoke Manufacture

Original joinery is among the first things lost in a renovation — and among the most costly to replace badly. We restore original windows and doors where they can be saved, and manufacture exact-match or architecturally-considered new joinery where they cannot. Every opening is an architectural statement.

For listed buildings, we work with English Heritage-approved joiners using traditional mortise-and-tenon construction, period glass, and historically accurate ironmongery. For contemporary commissions, we design bespoke steel, bronze, and hardwood systems from first principles.

  • Original sash window and casement restoration
  • Period ironmongery matching and sourcing
  • Draught-proofing and thermal upgrading of original frames
  • Bespoke hardwood windows — oak, accoya, iroko
  • Steel Crittall-style systems, designed to specification
  • Bronze and patinated metal door systems
  • Pivot doors, concealed frames, and flush threshold details
  • Listed building consent coordination for all joinery works
  • Secondary glazing for heritage buildings
Restoration Listed Buildings Bespoke Joinery Steel Systems Bronze Heritage Approved

07 — Bespoke Furniture
07 — Expanded Service

Bespoke Furniture
Design &
Commissioning

A house is not finished when the building work is done. The furniture that occupies a space is as much a part of the architecture as the walls themselves. We design and commission bespoke furniture as an integrated part of every interior commission — not as an afterthought, and not through a third party.

We also offer standalone furniture consultancy for clients who have completed builds and need considered pieces designed to their exact space, palette, and way of living.

  • Bespoke dining tables, desks, and case furniture
  • Built-in joinery: libraries, wardrobes, media units
  • Upholstered pieces: sofas, headboards, benches — to specification
  • Antique and vintage furniture sourcing and restoration
  • Loose furniture selection and procurement for full schemes
  • Hardware and finish specification: brass, bronze, blackened steel
  • Workshop visits and craft production oversight
  • Coordination with British furniture makers and European ateliers
