Common questions

What clients ask us.

Do I need planning permission?
It depends on the scope. Extensions over a certain size require planning. Internal refurbs usually don't. Listed buildings always need consent. We assess this in your first consultation and manage all applications on your behalf — included in our fee.
Can I live in the property during works?
For refurbs, it depends on the scope. For full renovations, we'd normally recommend moving out. We'll advise honestly based on your specific project and create a phased programme if staying is important.
How do fixed-price contracts work?
We price your project in full before work starts. Unless you change the brief, the price doesn't change. Variation orders require your written approval before any additional work proceeds. No surprises.
What warranties do you provide?
All new build work carries a 10-year structural warranty. Refurbs carry a 2-year defects liability period. We're also FMB members, which provides additional client protection through their dispute resolution service.
How long does a typical project take?
Flat refurb: 8–14 weeks. Full house renovation: 6–12 months. Extension: 4–8 months. New build: 12–24 months. These include design and planning. We provide a detailed programme before work starts.
Can you help with listed buildings?
Yes — this is one of our specialisms. We handle all listed building consent applications, work with heritage-approved craftsmen, and have extensive experience with English Heritage and local authority conservation officers.
