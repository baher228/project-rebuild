The Ardivia approach

More than extra space — better space.

A poorly designed extension adds square footage but ruins a house. A well-designed one transforms how the entire property lives. We approach every extension as an architectural problem first — how light moves, how the new space connects to the old, how the proportions read from the garden.

Because we design and build in-house, there's no gap between the architect's intent and what actually gets constructed. The team that draws your extension is accountable for building it — to the millimetre.

From planning permission and party wall agreements through structural steel and final decoration, it's one contract, one price, and one team responsible for the result.

Types of extension we build

Every configuration.

01
Single-storey rear
The classic kitchen-diner extension — opening the back of the house to the garden with glazing, roof lights, and seamless threshold details.
02
Double-storey
Adding space across two floors — typically a larger kitchen below and a bedroom or bathroom above, maximising the return on groundworks.
03
Side-return
Reclaiming the narrow alley beside a Victorian terrace to widen the ground floor — one of the highest-value moves in London housing.
04
Wrap-around
Combining a side-return and rear extension into a single L-shaped space that completely reconfigures the ground floor.
05
Over-structure & garage
Building above an existing single-storey element — garages, utility rooms — to add bedrooms without extending the footprint.
06
Glazed & orangery
Steel-framed glazed extensions, orangeries, and garden rooms — light-filled spaces with architectural roof structures.

The Ardivia approach

The cheapest square footage you'll ever add.

A loft conversion is almost always the highest-return way to add space to a house — the roof and walls already exist. But the difference between a cramped, awkward loft and one that feels like it was always a proper floor is entirely in the design.

We obsess over the things that make a loft work: head height at the right points, where the staircase lands, how the dormers are proportioned from the street, and how natural light reaches the centre of the plan.

Most of our loft conversions add a full bedroom suite with an en-suite bathroom, and typically increase property value by more than the cost of the works.

Types of loft conversion

Right for your roof.

01
Dormer
The most common conversion — a box dormer extends out from the roof slope to create full head height and floor area. Excellent value and flexibility.
02
Hip-to-gable
Replacing a sloping hip end with a vertical gable wall, dramatically increasing internal volume — ideal for semi-detached and end-of-terrace homes.
03
Mansard
The most extensive conversion — rebuilding the roof structure to near-vertical, maximising space across the full footprint. Common in conservation areas.
04
Rooflight (Velux)
Where head height already exists, simply adding rooflights and a floor — the most economical conversion, with minimal external alteration.
05
L-shaped dormer
Combining a main roof dormer with one over a rear addition — creating a generous multi-room conversion on period properties.
06
Bespoke & structural
Complex conversions involving roof raises, structural alteration, or terraces and balconies cut into the roofscape.
How it works

Up and running in weeks.

01
Survey & feasibility
Weeks 1–3
We assess your roof structure, head height, and the best conversion type. Most lofts fall under permitted development — we confirm this early.
02
Design & approvals
Weeks 3–8
Detailed design, party wall agreements, and building regulations application. Where planning is needed (mansards, conservation areas), we manage it.
03
Structure & build
Weeks 8–18
Steel beams, new floor joists, dormer construction, staircase, insulation, and weatherproofing — built by our team with minimal disruption to the floors below.
04
Fit-out & finish
Weeks 14–20
Plastering, en-suite installation, electrics, heating, and decoration. Bespoke storage built into the eaves to make use of every inch.
05
Sign-off & handover
Week 18+
Building control completion certificate, fire safety compliance, and handover. Your new floor, ready to move into.
Indicative investment
£1,500 – £2,500 / sq ft
All-in including design, structural work, build, en-suite, and finishing. Dormers and rooflight conversions sit at the lower end; mansards and structural roof works at the higher. Fixed price before work begins.

The Ardivia approach

The most complex residential work there is.

Basement excavation is the highest-risk, highest-skill work in residential construction — and where the gap between a good contractor and a poor one is widest. It demands serious structural engineering, faultless waterproofing, and meticulous management of party walls and neighbouring structures.

This is precisely the kind of work where our in-house model matters most. Our directly-employed teams and in-house structural engineers mean there is no subcontractor cutting corners on the things you'll never see — but that determine whether a basement stays dry for decades.

Every Ardivia basement carries full structural warranty and tanking guarantees, and we hold £10M professional indemnity cover.

What we deliver

Below ground, beautifully.

01
Full excavation
Digging out a new basement level beneath the existing footprint — including underpinning, structural slab, and retaining structure.
02
Cellar conversion
Converting an existing cellar into habitable space — lowering floors, tanking, and bringing head height and light up to standard.
03
Garden & lightwells
Excavated lightwells and sunken courtyards bringing natural daylight deep into basement levels — essential for liveable space.
04
Waterproofing & tanking
Multi-layer waterproofing systems with cavity drainage, sump pumps, and guaranteed tanking — the part that matters most.
05
Pools, cinemas & gyms
The most popular basement uses — swimming pools, home cinemas, gyms, wine cellars, and spa facilities, all designed in.
06
Party wall management
Full management of party wall awards and neighbour relations — the legal and diplomatic side that derails so many basement projects.
Indicative investment
£4,000 – £7,000 / sq ft
Basement work is the most variable of all — driven by ground conditions, water table, access, and structural complexity. We undertake detailed feasibility and ground investigation before providing a fixed price.

The Ardivia approach

Performance, without losing character.

The UK's housing stock is among the oldest and least efficient in Europe. New EPC regulations mean landlords and homeowners increasingly must upgrade — but a clumsy retrofit can damage a period building, cause damp, or strip out its character.

We take a whole-house, fabric-first approach: understanding how a building performs before specifying changes, so that insulation, ventilation, and heating work together rather than against each other. This is especially critical in older and listed buildings where the wrong intervention causes condensation and decay.

As an MCS-certified installer, we can access available grants and ensure all renewable installations are eligible for incentive schemes.

What we install

The full upgrade.

01
Insulation
Wall (internal & external), roof, floor, and loft insulation — specified to avoid cold bridging and interstitial condensation.
02
MVHR ventilation
Mechanical ventilation with heat recovery — fresh, filtered air without heat loss, essential for airtight modern retrofits.
03
Heat pumps
Air source and ground source heat pumps replacing gas boilers — designed and sized for the upgraded building fabric.
04
Solar PV & battery
Roof-integrated solar arrays and battery storage — generating and storing power on-site to cut bills and carbon.
05
Glazing upgrades
Secondary glazing, slim double-glazed units, and draught-proofing — thermal performance while retaining period windows.
06
EPC programmes
Full assessment, specification, and delivery of the works needed to reach EPC C or better — managed end-to-end.

The Ardivia approach

Comfort you feel, equipment you don't.

British summers are getting warmer, and high-end clients increasingly expect the same climate comfort at home that they have in their cars and offices. But conventional air conditioning is ugly — visible wall units, exposed pipework, and noise.

We design concealed, ducted systems integrated into ceiling voids, bulkheads, and joinery during the build — so cooling and heating reach every room through discreet linear grilles, with the plant hidden entirely from view and from earshot.

Because we design it alongside the architecture, there's no retrofitting, no compromise, and no ugly box on the wall — just perfect, silent, room-by-room climate control.

What we install

Whole-house comfort.

01
Ducted air conditioning
Concealed ducted systems delivering cooling and heating through discreet ceiling and wall grilles — invisible plant, even temperatures.
02
Multi-zone control
Independent climate control for every room or zone — so bedrooms, living spaces, and home offices each hold their own setting.
03
Air source heat pumps
Modern heat pump systems providing both heating and cooling from a single efficient unit — the sustainable route to year-round comfort.
04
Comfort cooling & MVHR
Integration with mechanical ventilation for fresh, filtered, temperature-controlled air throughout airtight modern homes.
05
Smart integration
Full integration with KNX, Crestron, and home automation — climate controlled by app, voice, or automated schedule.
06
Plant & condenser concealment
External condensers screened or housed within the landscape design; internal plant within dedicated, acoustically-isolated spaces.
Designed in, not bolted on
Part of your project
Climate control is best specified as part of a wider design-and-build or renovation project, where ductwork and plant can be concealed during construction. Standalone installations are available for completed properties.

Highgate Residence

The brief

A tired 1930s semi, reimagined as an 8,400 sq ft family home.

Our clients had bought a substantial but dated 1930s property in Highgate with the intention of creating their forever home. The house was structurally sound but poorly arranged, energy-inefficient, and far smaller than they needed for a family of five.

Ardivia delivered a complete transformation under a single contract: a double-storey rear extension, a full loft conversion creating a master suite, a basement excavation for a gym and cinema, and a comprehensive renovation and retrofit of the entire existing house.

Designed by our architecture team, engineered by our in-house structural engineers, and built by our own brigade — delivered on time and to a fixed price, with zero variation orders.
